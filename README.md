# 🇬🇧 English in the ADS Program at FATEC Taquaritinga

**An analysis of the English I to VI curriculum.** Monografia da disciplina de **Inglês** na **Fatec Taquaritinga**, escrita em inglês, que analisa como as seis disciplinas de Inglês do curso de **Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas** estão organizadas e se preparam o aluno para o trabalho de analista de sistemas.

📄 **Trabalho completo:** [`English_Curriculum_ADS_FATEC.docx`](English_Curriculum_ADS_FATEC.docx) (18 páginas, normas ABNT, com resumo e abstract)

---

## ❓ Research problem

> How is the English I to VI sequence organized in the ADS program at FATEC Taquaritinga, and how well does it prepare students for the communication needs of a systems analyst?

**Method:** qualitative, descriptive and **documentary analysis** of the objectives, syllabi and bibliography of the six courses.

---

## 📚 The six courses (240 classes)

| Course | Main focus | What the student learns to do |
|---|---|---|
| **English I** | Introduction, simple structures | Introduce yourself, describe places and people, fill in forms, take messages |
| **English II** | Consolidation of the basic level | Ask for permission, talk about work, compare, past experiences, answer the phone |
| **English III** | Expansion of basic structures | Politeness, opinions and requests, prices, data and graphs, simple business e-mails |
| **English IV** | Professional contexts | Discussions, negotiations, **job interviews**, reports, letters and **resumes** |
| **English V** | More complex structures | Argumentation, meetings and presentations, note-taking, academic articles |
| **English VI** | Oral skills and autonomy | Critical attitude, oral presentations, technical and academic texts, phone fluency |

### Progression of writing tasks

```
forms → simple correspondence → business e-mails → reports & resumes → business correspondence → technical & academic texts
 (I)          (II)                    (III)              (IV)                  (V)                        (VI)
```

## 🧪 Hypotheses and results

| Hypothesis | Result |
|---|---|
| **H1** – The sequence shows a clear and logical progression of skills | ✅ Confirmed |
| **H2** – Constant emphasis on oral communication and professional situations | ✅ Confirmed |
| **H3** – Limited work with IT-specific tasks and mostly older materials | ✅ Confirmed |

### 💪 Strengths
- Clear progression: each course consolidates or expands the previous one.
- Objectives connected to real professional situations (meetings, interviews, e-mails, phone calls).
- Pronunciation and oral skills present in all six courses.

### 🔧 Opportunities for improvement
- More practice with **IT-specific tasks**: technical documentation, error messages, forums.
- **Update the bibliography**, most of it published between 2007 and 2009, before cloud computing and AI became popular.
- Describe the **assessment methods** in the curriculum.
